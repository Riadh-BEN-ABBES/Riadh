About

Nanofabrication engineer at the Institute of NanoSciences of Paris (Sorbonne University / CNRS), I run a cleanroom platform: thin-film process development, equipment qualification and user training. My background spans process integration in advanced microelectronics (STMicroelectronics, CEA-Leti / LTM), III-V materials, and silicon photonic circuit design, from simulation to GDS layout.
