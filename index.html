1×4 CWDM MZI demultiplexer on SOI

Four-channel demultiplexer on SOI (220 nm): circuit design and simulation (Lumerical INTERCONNECT), GDS layout in KLayout / SiEPIC PDK, then fabrication (ANT NanoSOI MPW) and optical characterization. Cascaded-MZI architecture with broadband directional couplers.

1×4 CWDM demux · cascaded MZI · broadband directional couplers (bdc) · SOI λ = 1553 / 1555 / 1557 / 1559 nm

bdc = broadband directional coupler · Lc = 50.7 µm · gap = 200 nm · taper = terminated port · ΔL = 71.5 µm · 2ΔL = 143 µm

Mach-Zehnder interferometer on SOI

Design, simulation, fabrication and measurement of an unbalanced Mach-Zehnder interferometer on SOI. Spectral data were analyzed to extract the waveguide group index and chromatic dispersion. First silicon-photonics project (UBCx), establishing the design-to-measurement workflow later reused for the CWDM demultiplexer.
